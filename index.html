How to read this

Getting around: the bar at the top jumps to the team ratings, the player leaders, the top 100 players, or the GM board. East / West tabs above the team table filter to one conference; the filter box searches team names. Click a team name anywhere to open that team's own page, with its ratings, game log, and roster. Click a team row to see its game log and roster for the season right here.

AdjO / AdjD: points scored and allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponent. The small number under each value is that team's rank in the metric; AdjD rank 1 is the best defense. Net is AdjO minus AdjD, and its rank is the Rk column. AdjT is adjusted tempo, possessions per game. Conf is East or West. W-L is the record in the games these ratings are built from, so W+L equals GP.

ORtg (players) is a simplified approximation: points per 100 possessions used, with no assist credit, so it underrates playmakers. Read it with %Poss. RPR is our own single-number player rating, not KenPom's proprietary kPOY. Leaderboards only include players with at least 40% of their team's minutes, so a two-minute cameo cannot top the list.

Honest limits: about 15 games per team, so the order is still noisy. League-average prior only, no last-season prior. Box-score only: no tracking, no on/off, no opponent adjustment at player level. No contracts, salaries, age, or height/weight. Full method in the README.

Data: ESPN public scoreboard and box scores. Code and full CSVs: RD3-LIL/nba-ratings on GitHub. Built November 20, 2026.
