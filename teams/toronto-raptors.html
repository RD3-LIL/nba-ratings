TradeScore is QualityGain plus FitGain minus the availability discount. QualityGain is the target's RPR above the team's rotation-average RPR, scaled by his minutes, so he adds more to a weak rotation than to a strong one. FitGain is how much he lifts the skills this team actually needs above where the team already is, with diminishing returns, so the first rebounder counts more than the second, and it only counts gains. No player is named as displaced: that part was removed Nov 21, 2026. On this run QualityGain averages 0.06 and FitGain 0.06 across the top targets, so QualityGain drives the score more than fit does here. TradeScore is a fit ranking, not a prediction and not a pick.
