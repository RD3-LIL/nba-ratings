TradeScore is QualityGain plus twice FitGain minus the availability discount. QualityGain is the target's RPR above the team's rotation-average RPR, scaled by his minutes, so he adds more to a weak rotation than to a strong one. FitGain is how much he lifts the skills this team actually needs above where the team already is, with diminishing returns, so the first rebounder counts more than the second, and it only counts gains. Fit counts double because a generic quality ranking already exists on the GM board; this list is meant to be team-specific. No player is named as displaced: that part was removed Nov 21, 2026. On this run QualityGain averages 0.06 and FitGain 0.06 across the top targets, so fit drives the score more than quality does here. TradeScore is a fit ranking, not a prediction and not a pick. Salaries and ages are shown with the fit and do not change TradeScore.
